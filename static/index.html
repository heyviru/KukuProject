The challenge

Creators, platforms and educators face high costs, slow turnaround and inconsistent quality. Audio defines 50–60% of the experience—yet most of it is still manual.

Our approach

We combine DIA model narration from Nari Labs, OpenAI for story generation and timestamps, and HuggingFace Transformers for NLP on the text. The system mimics an editor’s workflow and renders reliably in Python.

  • Lifelike narration → parse text and words.

  • Mood‑aware BGM with tasteful fades and transitions.

  • Contextual ambience and SFX placed exactly on cue.

  • Final mix exported, share‑ready.
